# Second-screen design (dual-screen handhelds, "DS beta")

Status: **design approved for the battle menus on 2026-11-25; not implemented yet.** The code on this branch still
draws the previous version (the game's own menu images enlarged), which was rejected after testing on an AYN Thor.
No build is to be started until the owner authorises it.

`battle.png` / `battle.gif` are the approved mock-ups (command menu on the left, move menu on the right), generated by
`ds_mock.py` from `app/src/main/assets/pixelfonts.txt` and the game's text font.

## Battle menus: rules the implementation must follow

- Buttons are **drawn by code** at the size of the screen, in Fire Ash's button style: dark outline, white rim two
  pixels thick, bevelled two-tone body, soft shadow straight below. Every layer follows the same corner shape (the
  outer shape shrunk evenly), so the rim never breaks at the corners. Art grid: 206x180 "art pixels", 6 screen pixels
  each on the Thor's 1240x1080 screen.
- Button lettering (FIGHT, BAG, CANCEL...) uses the `cmd` pixel font, white with dark outline, drop shadow and the
  lower part of each letter slightly shaded.
- Texts in the game's font (move names, type, PP, effectiveness) are only drawn at **multiples of 13 px** (the font's
  own pixel grid), otherwise the strokes come out uneven. Base + shadow one font pixel to the right and below.
- **Command menu**: FIGHT large on top (no extra frame around it), BAG and POKéMON below at the sides, RUN smaller
  between them. A row of dots on top shows the state of the player's party (green ok, yellow status, grey fainted)
  and the opponent's on the right.
- **Move menu**: four framed buttons in the colour of the move's type with a light inner panel: name on top; below, the
  type as a tag in the type's colour (white text with shadow) and the PP on the right. Effectiveness is a band at the
  base of the button, part of it (green super effective, orange not very effective, grey no effect), only when there
  is something to say. CANCEL is a small centred button at the bottom; Mega Evolution goes to its right when available.
- **Cursor**: no coloured frame. The selected option bounces a couple of pixels in a loop and its shadow narrows and
  fades as it rises.
- Colours of types and buttons are to be taken from the game's own images at run time, not hard-coded.
- The "game-screen menu: hidden/shown" switch leaves this screen and moves to the launcher's settings.

## Game screen (top)

With the game's menu hidden, the message bar always shows "What will X do?" full width, also while choosing a move,
instead of an empty bar.

## Overworld (design only)

`overworld-home.png` (generated by `ds_mock_overworld.py`) was approved as an MVP on 2026-11-25: top bar with the
current location and the time; the party on the left (icon, name, level, HP bar, status / fainted tag); on the right
POKéMON, BAG, MAP, the profile button (labelled with the player's name, opens the Trainer Card) and SAVE.
SAVE writes the game (with confirmation, only while the game allows saving). Map, bag, Pokémon and profile are
read-only views in this first version; each gets its own mock-up before being built. The Pokémon icons in the mock-up
are placeholders: on the device they are the game's own.

`screen-map.png` (`ds_mock_flow.py`) shows how the screens connect and which ones still have no mock-up.
`overworld-map.png` and `overworld-route.png` are proposals under review (not approved yet): the region map with the
player's marker, and a route sheet opened from the map's place panel that lists the wild Pokémon per method (grass,
water, fishing...) with levels, encounter rate and time of day; species not seen yet show as a silhouette and "???".
Small buttons (tabs) use a one-pixel white rim so the lettering never touches the edge.

### Bag (approved 2026-11-25)

Mirroring the game's own bag screen onto the second screen was tried (build 179) and dropped: not viable. The bag is
instead **replicated natively** from the game's own images, font, colours and layout rules (`UI_Bag` in the game's
scripts), read from the player's copy of the game at run time. `ds_mock_bag.py <game folder> <out.png>` renders the
approved mock-up; its output is not committed because it is made of game art, and nothing of the game ships in the
repository or the APK.

- **Full screen, no bars.** The game's 512x384 bag background is cut along lines where it is uniform and those
  lines are repeated (never scaled): +64 px in the left panel, +44 in the item list, +124 of height in the upper
  part, +32 at the top of the description box. Logical canvas 620x540, shown at 2x (1240x1080).
- **Item list**: 11 rows visible instead of 7, the game's red cursor (extended the same way), quantities on the
  right, CLOSE BAG as the last entry (returns to the home screen).
- **Description box**: taller, its text starts 8 px below the box's top edge instead of on it. The item icon box
  grows from 64 to 88 px (same frame, extended) and is centred vertically; the icon is drawn at 3 screen pixels per
  pixel instead of 2.
- **Pocket buttons**: the strip of 20 px icons becomes two rows of four round touch buttons, drawn by code. The
  whole face of each button takes that pocket's two colours and middle band from the game's `icon_pocket.png`
  (colour row); on top goes only the drawing of that icon (its square background removed), at 3 screen pixels per
  pixel. States: idle (grey ring), selected (wider white ring), pressed (sunk two pixels, darkened to 72 %, no
  shadow); on release the pressed pocket becomes the selected one.
- **Optional larger pocket icons**: if the game folder has `Graphics/Pictures/Bag/icon_pocket_ds.png` (384x144:
  8 columns of 48x48 in pocket order, rows idle / selected / pressed, transparent background, drawn at the game's
  pixel size), the buttons use it instead. The launcher never ships such a file. Pocket 6 is Badges in Fire Ash.
- Read-only in this first version: browsing by touch or with the buttons; using items stays in the game's menu.
- Mock-ups must be judged at the screen's real resolution (1240x1080): halved previews show the 3x elements with
  uneven pixels.


## Region map (approved for now)

Mock-up: `ds_mock_map.py <game folder> <pixelfonts.txt> <out.png> [zoom]`.

- Top bar: region and place on the left, "Estás aquí" on the right.
- The map fills the area between the top bar and the buttons. Integer zoom only: 2x shows the whole region
  and is the default; 3x and 4x are centred on the player and clamped to the map edges. `+` / `-` buttons sit
  over the bottom right corner of the map; drag or D-pad to pan when zoomed in.
- Buttons: POKéMON SALVAJES (opens the route sheet) and VOLVER.
- The game's map image mixes pixel sizes (terrain at double pixels, buildings at single pixels) and carries the
  region logo; both come from the player's file and are shown as they are.

## Wild Pokémon sheet (approved)

Mock-up: `ds_mock_route.py <game folder> <pixelfonts.txt> <out.png>`, with the real table of Route 1.

- Fire Ash's `encounters.dat` only has Land, Cave, OldRod, GoodRod, SuperRod (and BugContest): no surfing and
  no time-of-day tables. Tabs are therefore HIERBA or CUEVA, and PESCA (split by rod); only the ones the map
  has are shown. No day/night tags.
- Entries of the same species are merged: rates added, level range widened. Sorted by rate.
- Each row: the game's icon at 2x, name, levels, rate bar and percentage. Five rows visible, the list scrolls.
- Species not seen in the Pokédex: flat silhouette of the icon and "???" as the name; levels and rate stay visible (decided).
- Rows are capsules with the same corner radius as the buttons; tab labels keep clear of the rim.

## Party (approved)

Mock-up: `ds_mock_party.py <game folder> <pixelfonts.txt> <out.png>`.

- Built from the game's party images (round panel for the first slot, rect for the rest, faint and selected
  variants, ball, HP bar, level and status images), the Pokémon icons and fonts, with `UI_Party`'s colours.
- Panels: widened to 296 px and made 138 px tall by repeating lines of the image, shown at 2x, two columns
  with the right one 16 px lower as in the game.
- Contents: everything inside a panel is shown at 3x and re-laid out (name on top, HP bar under it, level,
  status and HP numbers on the bottom row; HP written without spaces). Chosen over a variant with only icon
  and name at 3x. A straight 3x copy of the game's layout needs 768 px per panel.
- Bottom band: help line and VOLVER.

## Party actions (pending approval)

Mock-up: `ds_mock_party.py <game folder> <pixelfonts.txt> <out.png> actions`.

- After touching a Pokémon: its panel alone at the top, and under it the options the game gives for it
  (Summary, its field moves, Switch, Item or Mail) as a two-column grid of medium buttons in the approved
  style: smaller than the battle ones, one colour per kind. Rows are added as needed.
- The game's "Cancel" is the VOLVER button at the bottom right, as on the other screens; the help line asks
  what to do with that Pokémon.
