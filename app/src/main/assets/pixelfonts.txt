// Pixel fonts used to redraw lettering in game images (shared by the app and tools).
// Format: '@font <name> <width> <height>' then per glyph: a line 'char:<c>' followed by <height> rows ('#' = ink).
// Glyphs taller than <height> (accented capitals) have their extra rows drawn above the line.
// Glyphs may be narrower than <width> (proportional fonts): a glyph's width is the length of its rows.
// 'wide' follows the title screen prompt; 'cmd' follows the battle command buttons; 'dot9'/'dot5' follow the slot machine's lamp lettering; 'memo' follows Voltorb Flip's memo button; 'card' follows the trainer card title; 'tiny' follows the naming screen's key labels; 'bold8' follows the Pokédex tabs / SHIFT button lettering; 'type5' (short words) and 'type' (long words), 'status' and 'icon' follow the lettering of Essentials' type, status and battle status icons.
// Fonts may have lowercase glyphs ('naming'); missing ones fall back to uppercase, then to the plain letter without accent.
